Introduction to Treaty 1

Treaty 1 is the first of the numbered treaties signed in Canada. It was signed in 1871 between the Canadian government and the First Nations in southern Manitoba.

The treaty is important because it shows how land was shared and used in Manitoba, and it also affects relationships between the First Nations and the Canadian government today.

Learning about Treaty 1 helps us understand the history, land right and the promises that were made to the First Nations.
