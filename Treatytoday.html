Why treaty 1 still matters

Treaty 1 is still important today because it helps the First Nations people and the Government of Canada works together and respect each other. The treaty is not the only part of history because it still affects many people in Manitoba today. Treaty 1 is also important for things like education, heathcare, clean water, better homes for the First Nations communities. In schools, students will learn about Treaty 1 to understand the history of Indigenous people and Canada. Many people also celebrate Treaty Days and cultrural events to keep the traditions and stories alive. Treaty 1 teaches people about fairness, respect, and working together for better future.

Building on Treaty 1 Land

Building on Treaty Land means using land that is part of Treaty 1 for homes, schools, businesses, and community projects. Treaty 1 is an agreement between First Nations and the Government of Canada about how the land in shared and used.

Today, many First Nations communities in Manitoba use Treaty 1 land to bulid things that help their people, like housing, shops, and services. This helps create jobs.

It is really importamt to remember that this land is shared and has history, so poeple must respect the treaty building on it.
