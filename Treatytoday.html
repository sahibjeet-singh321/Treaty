Why treaty 1 still matters

Treaty 1 is still important today because it helps the First Nations people and the Government of Canada works together and respect each other. The treaty is not the only part of history because it still affects many people in Manitoba today. Treaty 1 is also important for things like education, heathcare, clean water, better homes for the First Nations communities. In schools, students will learn about Treaty 1 to understand the history of Indigenous people and Canada. Many people also celebrate Treaty Days and cultrural events to keep the traditions and stories alive. Treaty 1 teaches people about fairness, respect, and working together for better future.

Indigenous Culture Today.

Indigenous Culture Today includes the traditions, beliefs, languages, and ways of life Indigenous people that are still alive today. Many Indigenous people continue to share stories, celebrate special events, they wear traditional clothing and they also make art also they speak their traditional languages.

Today, Indigenous culture is growing and changing while staying connectes to the past. Many young Indigenous people are learning their languages and traditions from there Elders and the community leaders. Indigenous Culture is an important part of Canada's identity and helps make the country more respectful.

Learning about Indigenous Culture helps people understand the importance of respect, kindness and the community. It also helps Canadians learn the history of the land they live in.

Building on Treaty 1 land

Building on Treaty 1 land means using the land that is part of Treaty 1 for homes, schools, businesses, roads and other projects. Treaty 1 is an agreement between the First Nations and the Government of Canada about how that is shared and used.

Today, many First Nations people in Manitoba use Treaty 1 land to bulid things that help their people, like housing, stores, offices, health centres. These projects help create jobs and improve life for many people.

Some First Nations also work with businessess and cities to create new job opportunities. This help communities earn money, support local workers, and grow stronger for the future.

It is important to remember that Treaty 1 land has a deep history and meaning. People should respect the land. Treaty 1 teaches people to share the land fairly and work together peacefully.
